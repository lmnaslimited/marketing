### The Challenge of Cross-Company Quotations

Imagine this: a customer approaches your sales team with an inquiry for products from two different sister companies within your corporate group. The sales team is ready to meet the challenge, but the reality is that each company operates in its own silo. This setup requires separate logins, different quoting systems, and inconsistent terms and conditions, resulting in confusion and delays. Multiple quotes may be issued for a single inquiry, each with varying terms, complicating the process for the customer and the sales team.

### The Usual Process

Traditionally, the central sales team works on deals for a single company at a time, preparing quotes exclusively for that company's products. This involves logging in to each company’s quoting system separately and issuing quotes for products within that entity's scope. The lack of integration means that the team often faces unnecessary roadblocks when handling cross-company requests.

### What the Sales Team Desires

The sales team seeks a unified platform where all essential details—such as customer information, pricing conditions, and product specifics—are readily accessible. For Engineer-to-Order requirements, they need the ability to design on the fly and quickly obtain comprehensive costing details, including raw material expenses, product costs, margins, and overheads. This streamlined approach would enable them to deliver clear, timely quotes and significantly increase the likelihood of closing deals.
